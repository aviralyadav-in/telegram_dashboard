1. Telegram Destination
The connected bot will publish the selected deals to this destination.
2. Date Filter
Leave empty if you want deals from all dates.
3. Price Range
Only deals whose stored price falls inside this range will be selected.
4. Rating Filter
Example: 4 means 4.0 and above.
5. Publishing Settings
Maximum number of matching deals to publish.
Example: 10 means one deal every 10 seconds.
Example
Channel: Myntra Deals
Price: ₹500 – ₹2000
Rating: 4+ ⭐
Deals: 5
Interval: 10 seconds

Matching deals will be published automatically:
Deal 1 → 10 sec → Deal 2 → 10 sec → Deal 3 → 10 sec → Deal 4 → 10 sec → Deal 5
You do not need to press the normal Publish button for every deal. Once this schedule starts, the bot handles the publishing automatically.
